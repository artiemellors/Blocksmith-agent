Blocksmith

AI-powered training block generation built specifically for HYROX athletes.

What is Blocksmith?

Blocksmith generates structured, periodized training blocks tailored to your physiological markers, weekly schedule, and race category. Input your threshold paces and heart rate data, configure your week — and receive fully prescribed sessions: sets, reps, paces, HR zones, rest periods, and progression rationale for every workout.

Training Phases

Each block focuses on a single periodization phase:

  • Base (General Preparation) — High volume, low specificity. Build your aerobic engine and structural strength. Running is mostly Zone 2, strength focuses on volume accumulation.
  • Build (Specific Preparation) — Moderate volume, higher intensity. Threshold development with T1/T2 running. Station work at race weights with compromised running to build HYROX-specific fitness.
  • Peak (Competition) — Lower volume, very high intensity. Race pace or faster running, full race simulation work. Strength shifts to intensity over volume.
  • Taper — Maximum freshness. Cut volume 40–60% but keep intensity sharp. Leave each session feeling better than you started.

Our Recommendations

  • Focused 4–6 week blocks — We recommend each block targets a single training phase for concentrated adaptation. String together 2–3 blocks to complete a full training phase.
  • Deload weeks — Each block includes an optional deload week, and we highly recommend taking it. Volume drops ~40% while keeping movement quality sharp — this is when adaptation consolidates.
  • Polarized intensity distribution — Sessions follow a 60–70% easy, ~20% moderate, ~10% hard distribution by training time. No back-to-back high-intensity (Z4–Z5) days.
  • Race weight, always — Station work uses official HYROX race weights for your category. Progression happens through volume (more reps, longer distances) and density (less rest) — not by reducing load.
  • Individual adaptation — Your thresholds, available equipment, injury history, and schedule constraints shape every block.

How It Works

Select your training phase and race category (Men's/Women's Open or Pro), enter your physiological data (HR max, T1/T2 threshold paces), and configure your week structure — rest days, double days, long run day. Blocksmith builds each week progressively with phase-appropriate volume increases, then backs off in the deload week to let adaptation consolidate.

Built for athletes who want smarter training, not just more training.
